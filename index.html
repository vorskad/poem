A Strong Mind, A Silent Flame

In the quiet corners of the mind so strong,
Where thoughts roam free and do not belong,
There lies a depth, both fierce and wide,
A mystery hidden, none can divide.

Through the miles, through the silent space,
A passion lingers, time cannot erase.
Though distance may stretch, hearts remain near,
Connected by love, felt crystal clear.

A mind that holds both wisdom and flame,
A heart that beats without a name.
In every word, a silent plea,
To bridge the gap, to simply be.

Mystery dances in the spaces we share,
A love that whispers, bold and rare.
No distance can sever the bond so tight,
For in this love, we find our light.
